Chore Scheduler — minimal mono

Every screen in the app flow, in one quiet design language, in light and dark. Colour is used only for people. Everything else is type, hairlines and space. Every screen has a visible way to change something, so the solver never feels like a black box.

Quiet by defaultGreyscale, hairline rules, one thin numeral per screen. The only colour is each roommate's dot, so "who" reads instantly.
Always an exit to editAn underlined text action (Edit, Swap, Move, Pin) on every screen. Control is always one tap away, never shouting.
Explain, don't hideWhen the solver bends a rule, the app says who, when and why, in the same calm voice.
Your overrides stickManual swaps can be pinned, so the next re-plan keeps them instead of quietly undoing your choice.

Widgets

Home screen, lock screen, and how to configure them

Small: glance

2 × 2 · 158 pt
  • Next for you: your next task and how far away it is
  • Today: house progress ring and what's left
  • Rest: days until your next task

Small: act & fairness

2 × 2 · interactive
  • Mark done: tick your task without opening the app
  • Fairness: this month's task counts per person

Medium

4 × 2 · 338 × 158 pt
  • Today list: every task due today, tappable checkboxes
  • This week: seven days, one dot per assignment

Medium & strip

4 × 2 · 4 × 1
  • Workload: tasks per person, plus the spread
  • Strip: one-line Today for Android-style 4×1 slots

Large

4 × 4 · 338 × 354 pt
  • Week agenda: the whole week; today is marked and past days fade

Large: fridge board

4 × 4 · interactive
  • The paper grid on your home screen
  • Tap a name to strike it through
  • Great on a kitchen tablet's home screen

Lock screen

circular · rectangular · inline
  • Inline: next to the date
  • Circular: house progress, days until yours
  • Rectangular: next task, what's left today

long-press → edit
  • Show me or the whole house
  • Range: today, 3 days, week
  • Tap action: open, or mark done
